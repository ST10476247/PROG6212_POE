Data Dictionary & Relationship Rules

1. ORGANISER Parent Entity

Represents event organizing bodies (e.g. CMA, Cape Town Cycle Tour Trust, Soweto AC).

AttributeTypeKey
OrganiserIDIntPK
OrganizationNameString-
ContactEmailString-
ProvinceString-
Cardinality: 1 Organiser ── (1:N) ── N Events
2. EVENT Core Entity

South African road running, cycling, or walking events (Comrades, Soweto Marathon, etc.).

AttributeTypeKey
EventIDIntPK
OrganiserIDIntFK
EventNameString-
LocationString-
Cardinality: 1 Event ── (1:N) ── N Categories
3. CATEGORY Sub-Entity

Distance options, entry fees in ZAR, start times, and cutoffs (e.g., 89.8km Down Run, 109km Peninsula).

AttributeTypeKey
CategoryIDIntPK
EventIDIntFK
DistanceKmDecimal-
EntryFeeZARDecimal-
Cardinality: 1 Category ── (1:N) ── N Entries
4. PARTICIPANT Athlete Entity

Registered runner/cyclist profile containing SA ID/Passport, club affiliation, and emergency contacts.

AttributeTypeKey
ParticipantIDIntPK
FirstName / LastNameString-
SAIDOrPassportString-
ClubNameString-
Cardinality: 1 Participant ── (1:N) ── N Entries
5. ENTRY Associative M:N

Resolves Many-to-Many relationship between Athletes and Categories. Holds Bib Number & Payment info.

AttributeTypeKey
EntryIDIntPK
ParticipantIDIntFK
CategoryIDIntFK
BibNumberString-
Cardinality: 1 Entry ── (1:0..1) ── 1 Result
6. RESULT Race Day Timing

Official timing results, gun time, chip time, overall/category/gender rankings, and status.

AttributeTypeKey
ResultIDIntPK
EntryIDIntFK (Unique)
GunTime / ChipTimeTimeSpan-
OverallRankInt-
Cardinality: 1 Result belongs to exactly 1 Entry
